- **2026 -- MOSbius Twist (V3) chips are here.** The first Twist (V3) chips came back from the first [wafer.space](https://wafer.space) run and are passing their first tests. Twist (V3) is the new baseline MOSbius chip and is designed to be pin-compatible with Twist Classic (V1). Coming soon, stay tuned! See [The MOSbius Chip Family](chip-family).
- **2026 -- MOSbius Toggle (V2) is up and running.** The advanced Toggle (V2) chip has been brought up and programmed as switched-capacitor circuits. Samples are available for collaborators who want to help develop experiments and materials. See [The MOSbius Chip Family](chip-family).
- **2026 -- MOSbius Twist Classic (V1) is sold out.** Thank you to all the students and instructors who used it! The materials on this website remain available and will carry over to Twist (V3).
