- [Peter Kinget](https://www.ee.columbia.edu/~kinget) is the project lead
- _MOSbius Twist Classic_ (aka V1)
    - [Longyi Li](https://www.linkedin.com/in/longyi-li/)
    led the chip design and tape-out; [Yunfan Gao](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yunfan-gao/), [Zhuo Chen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zhuochen0528/), [Petar Barac](https://www.linkedin.com/in/petar-b-1b0497106/), [Ray Xu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rui-xu-66b86316/), and [Hongzhe Jiang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hongzhejiang/)
    contributed to the chip design and tape-outs
    - [Cade Gleekel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cade-gleekel/)
    led the development of the PCB, designed the logo, and created several demonstration experiments; Nico de la Cruz and [Rosnel Alejandro Leyva-Cortes](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rosnel-leyva-cortes/)
    assembled some of the MOSbius PCBs
    - [Yuechen He](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yuechenhe/) and [Jingde Hu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jingde-hu/)
    helped develop the lab experiments
    - [Jianxun Zhu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/coiughdpjlpse797eloa) updated the LTspice library, created the Raspberry Pi Pico flow, and developed circuit demonstrators; he also made the beautiful MOSbius logo animation
    - [Kaylie Kim](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kaylie-kim-69bbb5384/) designed the MOSbius Tester in Summer 2025 and tested the PCBs before shipping them across the world.
- _MOSbius Toggle_ (aka V2)
    - [Xianglin Pu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/xianglinpu/), [Jingru Li](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jl-jingru-li/), [Qizhe Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/qizhe-wu-589a05324/), [Yuntao Guo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yuntaoguo/), [Jingde Hu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jingde-hu/), and [Andrew Chon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrewwchonn/)
    designed the chip in 180nm CMOS in Spring 2025: Xianglin Pu also designed the _Toggle_ PCB and, with Jingde Hu, brought up and tested the chip.
    - [Jianxun Zhu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/coiughdpjlpse797eloa) developed the _Toggle_ LTspice library and Raspberry Pi Pico programming flow.
- _MOSbius Twist_ (aka V3)
    - [Andrew Chon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrewwchonn/), [Jianxun Zhu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/coiughdpjlpse797eloa), [Luighi Viton-Zorrilla](https://www.linkedin.com/in/luighiviton/), and [Xianglin Pu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/xianglinpu/)
    designed the chip in GF180MCU in Fall 2025 for the first wafer.space run. Nico de la Cruz designed the first version of the _Twist_ adapter PCB.
- _MOSbius PLL_
    - [Andrew Chon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrewwchonn/), [Xianglin Pu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/xianglinpu/), and [Qizhe Wu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/qizhe-wu-589a05324/)
    designed the chip in GF180MCU during the Summer 2025 IEEE SSCS Chipathon, mentored by Kumar Lakshmikumar and Jianxun Zhu.
